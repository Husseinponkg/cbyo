About Us

Building the capacity of youth to become productive, responsible, and successful members of society.

Who We Are

Capacity Building for Youth Organization (CBYO) is a youth-focused organization dedicated to empowering young people through education, leadership training, skills development, entrepreneurship, and community engagement programs.

Our Mission

To build the capacity of youth by providing opportunities, knowledge, and practical skills that enable them to become productive, responsible, and successful members of society.

Our Vision

A society where every young person has access to the resources, education, and support needed to achieve their full potential.

Our Core Values

  • Integrity
  • Innovation
  • Teamwork
  • Leadership
  • Community Service
  • Accountability

Our Activities

  • Youth Leadership Training
  • Entrepreneurship Workshops
  • Community Development Projects
  • Career Guidance and Mentorship
  • Digital Skills and ICT Training
  • Environmental Conservation Programs
